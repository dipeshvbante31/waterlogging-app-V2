Purpose and Users

Waterlogging Watch aims to provide an accessible indication of whether current and forecast rainfall may increase waterlogging risk. It uses weather information and machine learning to support awareness and informed decision-making.

The application is intended for residents, commuters, students, and people interested in applied machine learning. It is an informational screening tool and does not replace official weather alerts, flood warnings, or emergency instructions.

Technology Stack

  • Python and FastAPI for backend development
  • Jinja2 for server-rendered HTML templates
  • HTML5, CSS3, and vanilla JavaScript for the frontend
  • Pandas and NumPy for data processing
  • Scikit-learn and Joblib for machine learning and model persistence
  • SQLAlchemy with SQLite or PostgreSQL for prediction history
  • Open-Meteo APIs for weather forecasts and historical weather data

Data Sources

  • Open-Meteo: Provides weather forecasts, live weather information, and historical weather data.
  • NYC Open Data: Provides 311 service requests used as an observed-outcome data source in the training pipeline.

Data availability, licensing, and provider terms should be verified before commercial or large-scale use.

Limitations and Responsible Use

  • Weather data does not provide street-level measurements of water accumulation.
  • Drainage capacity, blocked drains, terrain, tides, and road closures may not be represented in the predictions.
  • Service requests may reflect reporting behaviour as well as actual waterlogging conditions.
  • A model trained on one geographical area may not generalise reliably to another area.
  • Historical validation results do not guarantee future prediction accuracy.
  • Always follow official weather alerts, local authority advisories, and emergency instructions.
